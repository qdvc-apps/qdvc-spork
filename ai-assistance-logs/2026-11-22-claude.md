**What went wrong:** `context(for:)` returns a tuple with labels, `(errors:, offline:)`. When I wrote `lastFetch?.context(for: path) ?? ([:], [])`, Swift used the fallback's unlabeled tuple type for the result. The labels were silently dropped, so `ctx.errors` stopped existing.
